      About this image converter

      Need a JPG as a PNG for transparency or editing, a PNG as a JPG to shrink it for email, or a WebP for a faster website? This free converter switches images between JPG, PNG and WebP in one click, with batch conversion so you can drop in a whole folder of screenshots or photos and convert them all at once.

      Conversion runs on an HTML canvas entirely on your device — the image is decoded and re-encoded locally, so there is no upload queue and no file-size cap imposed by a server. Most online converters force an upload first; here your files never leave the browser, which matters for client work, screenshots with personal data, and anything you would rather not hand to a stranger’s server.

      How to use

      1. Drop one or more images into the upload box (or click to browse).
      2. Choose the output format: PNG, JPG or WebP.
      3. Click Convert images — each file converts in seconds.
      4. Download each converted file individually from the results list.

      Limitations

      • Converting JPG to PNG will not restore quality lost to JPG compression, and PNG files are usually larger than the JPG they came from.
      • JPG output cannot preserve transparency — transparent areas become black unless the page flattens them; use PNG or WebP to keep transparency.
      • Animated GIFs convert as a single still frame; animation is not preserved.
      • Very old browsers may not support WebP output; the tool will report an encoding error in that case.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I convert JPG to PNG?

      Drop the JPG into the tool above, keep “Convert to” on PNG, and click Convert images. The PNG downloads instantly — no signup, no upload. The same page converts PNG back to JPG or either format to WebP.

      Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

      No. JPG compression discards detail permanently, and converting to PNG cannot bring it back. Convert to PNG when you need lossless editing or transparency, not to gain quality.

      What is the difference between JPG, PNG and WebP?

      JPG is best for photos (small files, no transparency). PNG is lossless and keeps transparency (larger files, ideal for graphics). WebP gives near-PNG quality at near-JPG sizes and is supported by all modern browsers.

      Are my images uploaded anywhere?

      No. Conversion happens 100% in your browser via the canvas API. Files never leave your device.
