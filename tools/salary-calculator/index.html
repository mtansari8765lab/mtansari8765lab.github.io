About this salary calculator

Job offers come in different units — one quotes $52,000 a year, another $25 an hour — and comparing them needs a common denominator. This calculator converts between annual, monthly, weekly and hourly pay using your actual hours per week (the classic 2,080-hour year assumes 40 hours × 52 weeks, but part-time and 37.5-hour schedules are fully supported).

Remember this is gross pay before tax. Two jobs with identical gross figures can leave very different amounts in your pocket depending on tax jurisdiction, benefits and deductions — use the conversions to compare offers, not to budget take-home pay.

How to use

  1. Enter your pay, say whether it is annual or hourly, set your hours per week and currency.
  2. Press Convert for the full breakdown.
  3. Use the Overtime estimate box to see effective annual pay when you regularly work past 40 hours.

Limitations

  • Gross pay only — taxes, social security, retirement and insurance are not deducted.
  • Assumes 52 working weeks; unpaid leave is not subtracted.
  • Overtime rules vary by country and contract — 1.5× beyond 40h is the common default, not a legal guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert annual salary to hourly pay?

Divide the annual salary by total hours worked per year. For a standard 40-hour week that is 40 × 52 = 2,080 hours, so $52,000 a year is $25 an hour. Enter your actual hours per week for an accurate figure.

Why does my actual paycheck differ from the calculator?

This calculator converts gross pay. Your take-home pay is lower after income tax, social security, retirement contributions, insurance premiums and unpaid leave — which vary by country, state and employer.

How is overtime pay calculated?

Hours beyond 40 per week are paid at a multiplier — commonly 1.5× ("time and a half"). The overtime section estimates your effective annual pay at your hours and multiplier.

Can I use this for part-time or custom hours?

Yes. Change "hours per week" to your actual schedule (e.g. 37.5 or 20) and all conversions adjust — no 40-hour assumption is forced.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The calculator runs 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your numbers never leave your device, are never stored, and never sent to any server.
