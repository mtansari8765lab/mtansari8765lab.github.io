      About this image compressor

      Email attachment limits, upload forms capped at 2MB, and slow portfolio pages all punish oversized photos. This free image compressor shrinks JPG, PNG and WebP images two ways: pick a quality level for a quick shrink, or — the trick most tools skip — set an exact target file size like 100KB and let the tool hunt the best quality that fits. That exact-size mode is what forums, marketplaces and application portals actually ask for.

      Everything runs on a canvas in your browser: the image is re-encoded locally at your chosen quality, and the target-size mode binary-searches the highest quality that lands under your KB limit. Unlike TinyPNG-style services there is no upload, no queue and no monthly cap — and the privacy story is stronger, because your photos are never transmitted anywhere.

      How to use

      1. Drop one or more images into the upload box (or click to browse).
      2. Choose By quality and move the slider, or To exact file size and type a KB target (e.g. 100).
      3. Pick JPG or WebP output (PNG is lossless, so it cannot shrink by quality).
      4. Click Compress images — the results show before/after sizes and the percentage saved.

      Limitations

      • Compression is lossy: pushing quality very low or targeting a tiny KB size will visibly degrade the image, especially text and sharp edges.
      • PNG output is lossless — it will not shrink meaningfully by “quality”; use JPG or WebP for real compression.
      • Exact-size mode cannot go below the encoder’s minimum quality; extremely small targets on huge photos may undershoot slightly or fail.
      • Animated GIFs are compressed as a single still frame.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I compress an image to exactly 100KB?

      Choose “To exact file size”, type 100, and click Compress images. The tool automatically finds the highest JPEG/WebP quality whose output stays under 100KB — a feature most free compressors do not offer.

      Will compressing reduce image quality?

      It depends on the setting. Quality 80 looks identical to the original for almost all photos while cutting the file roughly in half. Below ~50 you will start to see artifacts in skies, gradients and text. The results list shows exactly what you saved so you can judge the tradeoff.

      Why is there no upload?

      Compression happens 100% in your browser using the canvas API. Nothing is uploaded, so there are no queues, no accounts and no privacy concerns — unlike most online compressors, which require sending your photos to their servers.

      JPG or WebP — which compresses better?

      WebP typically produces files ~25–35% smaller than JPG at the same visual quality and is supported by all modern browsers. Choose WebP unless you need maximum compatibility with old software.
