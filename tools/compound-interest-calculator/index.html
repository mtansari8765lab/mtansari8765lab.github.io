About this compound interest calculator

Compound interest is interest earning interest. In year one, a 5% rate on $1,000 adds $50. By year ten, the same 5% applies to a much larger balance, so the dollar gains accelerate — the growth curve bends upward. The table above makes this visible: early rows are mostly your own contributions, later rows are mostly interest.

Two levers matter more than anything: time and contributions. Doubling the rate helps, but starting ten years earlier or adding a monthly contribution usually wins. Try the defaults ($1,000 at 5% for 10 years with $100/month) against the same setup with $0 monthly contributions to see the difference.

How to use

  1. Enter the starting amount, annual rate, time horizon and compounding frequency.
  2. Add an optional monthly contribution.
  3. Press Calculate growth for the future value, total contributions, interest earned and a year-by-year table.

Limitations

Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest earned on both your original money and the interest already added. Over time the growth curve bends upward — the later years earn far more than the early ones, which is why starting early matters more than the exact rate.

How do monthly contributions change the result?

Dramatically. Regular contributions usually dominate the final balance for most savers — the growth table shows your contributions and interest separately so you can see which does the heavy lifting.

What does compounding frequency mean?

How often interest is calculated and added: yearly, half-yearly, quarterly, monthly or daily. More frequent compounding grows slightly faster, but the difference between monthly and daily is small compared to the rate and the time horizon.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is an educational estimate tool, not financial advice. It ignores taxes, fees and inflation, and past market returns do not predict future results. Talk to a qualified professional before investing.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The calculator runs 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your numbers never leave your device, are never stored, and never sent to any server.
