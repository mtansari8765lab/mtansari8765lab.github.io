About this case converter

Pasted text arrives in the wrong case all the time: headings shouted in ALL CAPS from a PDF, titles typed entirely in lowercase, or a paragraph that needs proper sentence capitalization before publishing. Fixing it by hand is tedious and easy to get wrong. This free case converter changes the capitalization of any text instantly, with six modes covering every common need — from document headings to social media styling.

The details are handled properly, not approximately. Title Case capitalises the first letter of each word while treating apostrophes inside words sensibly, so "don't" becomes "Don't" and "o'clock" becomes "O'clock" — avoiding the "Don'T" error that cruder converters produce. Sentence case lowercases everything first, then capitalises the first letter after each sentence-ending mark, which is ideal for normalising pasted paragraphs. Capitalized Case uppercases only the very first letter of the whole text, and aLtErNaTiNg case gives you the stylised alternating look in one click.

Conversion happens instantly on your device, and you can copy the result or download it as a .txt file — handy for moving cleaned-up text into documents, emails or content management systems.

How to use

  1. Paste or type your text into the input box.
  2. Click a case button — UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, Capitalized case or aLtErNaTiNg.
  3. The converted result appears immediately below.
  4. Use Copy to copy it, Download .txt to save it as a file, or Clear to start over.

Limitations

  • Title Case follows standard word-boundary rules; style guides differ on small words (a, the, of) and this tool capitalises them like any other word.
  • Sentence case relies on . ! ? … to find sentence ends — abbreviations like "e.g." may trigger unwanted capitals.
  • Locale-specific casing (e.g. Turkish dotted İ) follows standard Unicode rules, not locale-specific ones.
  • Very large pastes (megabytes of text) may take a moment; the tool is designed for documents, not bulk data processing.

Frequently asked questions

What is Title Case and how does it handle apostrophes?

Title Case capitalises the first letter of each word. This converter treats apostrophes inside words sensibly: "don't" becomes "Don't" and "o'clock" becomes "O'clock", instead of the common error "Don'T".

What is the difference between Sentence case and Capitalized Case?

Sentence case capitalises the first letter after every sentence-ending mark (. ! ?), so a whole paragraph reads naturally. Capitalized Case capitalises only the very first letter of the text and lowercases everything else.

Can I download the converted text?

Yes. Use the Download .txt button to save the converted result as a plain-text file on your device. Copy puts it on your clipboard instead.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion runs 100% in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
