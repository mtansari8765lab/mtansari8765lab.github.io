Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Short version: MaxTools itself collects nothing about you directly. There are no accounts, and everything you type into a tool is processed entirely in your browser — your text, files and passwords never leave your device. We do use Google Analytics to count visits and see which tools are popular, and the site is approved for Google AdSense, which serves ads to keep the tools free. Those Google services set their own cookies and process technical data (such as IP address and device identifiers) as described below.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for, and do not store, your name, email address or any account details — there is no signup.
  • We do not log, transmit or store anything you type into a tool. Tool input is processed locally by JavaScript and disappears when you leave the page.
  • This "we do not collect" promise covers MaxTools itself. Third-party services on the site — Google Analytics and Google AdSense — process technical data as described in the sections below, and our hosting provider necessarily sees standard server-log data.

Analytics cookies

  • We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-T2LH1SV8Y5) to understand aggregate usage (pages visited, approximate location, device type). It sets first-party cookies (such as _ga) that expire after up to 2 years.
  • We do not use Analytics data to identify you, and we do not combine it with anything you type into the tools.
  • Your consent choice: when you first visit, a cookie banner asks for your consent. Choosing Accept enables these analytics cookies; choosing Decline keeps them off entirely. Your choice is remembered on your device, and clearing this site's stored data will ask you again. This banner covers analytics consent only — it does not cover advertising consent (see "Advertising consent" below).
  • You can also opt out with your browser's cookie controls or Google's opt-out add-on.

What technically happens when you visit

  • Like every website, our hosting provider necessarily sees standard web-server log data (such as IP address, requested page and time) in order to deliver the pages. We do not combine this with anything else, and we do not use it to identify you.
  • The site loads third-party scripts from Google only: Google Analytics (googletagmanager.com) for aggregate visit counting, and Google AdSense (pagead2.googlesyndication.com) to serve ads. No other third-party scripts, fonts, frameworks or CDNs are loaded.

Advertising

MaxTools is approved for Google AdSense and uses Google advertising services, including Auto Ads, to serve ads that keep the tools free. Ads may not appear on every page or every visit.

  • Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP address and similar technologies to deliver ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see the same ad, and help prevent invalid ad activity.
  • Whether you see personalized or non-personalized ads depends on your location and your consent choices. You can control ad personalization in Google Ads Settings.
  • Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Advertising consent — current gap

The cookie banner on this site controls analytics consent only: it sets analytics_storage and does not set advertising-consent signals (ad_storage or ad_personalization). No Google-certified consent management platform (CMP), such as Google Funding Choices, is installed on this site, and no advertising-consent choice is collected or recorded here. This banner therefore does not, by itself, satisfy consent requirements for personalized advertising in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. Until a certified CMP is added, advertising consent for visitors in those regions is not collected through this site. This is a known gap we disclose here rather than claim otherwise.
