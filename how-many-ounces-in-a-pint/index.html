How Many Ounces in a Pint?

A US liquid pint contains 16 fluid ounces. An imperial pint contains 20 imperial fluid ounces. Use the calculator below to convert pints to ounces and ounces back to pints.

Quick answer: 1 US liquid pint = 16 fl oz. 1 imperial pint = 20 fl oz.

Formula

For US liquid pints, multiply pints by 16. For imperial pints, multiply pints by 20.

US fluid ounces = pints × 16

Imperial fluid ounces = pints × 20

To convert back, divide fluid ounces by 16 for US liquid pints or by 20 for imperial pints.

FAQs

How many ounces are in a pint?

A US liquid pint has 16 fluid ounces. An imperial pint has 20 imperial fluid ounces.

How many fluid ounces are in 2 pints?

2 US liquid pints equal 32 fluid ounces. 2 imperial pints equal 40 imperial fluid ounces.

Is a pint always 16 ounces?

No. 16 fluid ounces is the US liquid pint. The imperial pint used in the UK is 20 imperial fluid ounces.

How many ounces are in a US dry pint?

A US dry pint is about 18.62 US fluid ounces by volume, but dry pints are used for dry measure and are not the same as liquid pints.

How do I convert pints to ounces?

For US liquid pints, multiply pints by 16. For imperial pints, multiply pints by 20.
