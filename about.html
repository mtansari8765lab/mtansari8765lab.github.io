About QuickTools

QuickTools is a collection of free online utilities — text tools, converters and calculators — built around one simple principle: your data should never have to leave your device to count words, convert a file or generate a password.

Most free tool sites process your input on their servers. That means your text, files or passwords travel across the internet, sit on someone else's computer, and may be logged. QuickTools does the opposite. Every tool is written in plain JavaScript and runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend, no database, and no account system. When you close the tab, your input is gone.

This approach has practical benefits beyond privacy. The tools are fast — results update as you type with no network round-trips. They work offline once the page has loaded. And they cost nothing to run, which is why every tool here is free with no usage limits and no signup.

What we build

We focus on small, genuinely useful utilities that do one job well: counting and cleaning text, converting between formats, and everyday calculations. Each tool is tested, documented with its limitations, and kept free of clutter — no fake download buttons, no misleading ads near the action buttons, no dark patterns.

How the site is funded

QuickTools is free to use and will stay that way. The site is supported by advertising shown around the tools — never by charging for features, selling data (we collect none), or limiting usage. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what little information exists here.
