Formula

There are 16 ounces in one pound. To convert ounces to pounds, divide the ounce value by 16.

pounds = ounces ÷ 16

For example, 12 oz ÷ 16 = 0.75 lb, and 32 oz ÷ 16 = 2 lb. To convert back, multiply pounds by 16.

Practical examples

FAQs

Does 16 oz make 1 lb?

Yes. There are exactly 16 ounces in 1 pound, so 16 oz ÷ 16 = 1 lb.

Is 8 oz the same as 1 lb?

No. 8 oz is half a pound: 8 ÷ 16 = 0.5 lb.

Is 32 oz 1 lb?

No. 32 oz equals 2 lb because 32 ÷ 16 = 2.

How many pounds is 1 oz?

1 oz equals 0.0625 lb, which is one sixteenth of a pound.

How do I convert lbs back to oz?

Multiply pounds by 16. For example, 2.5 lb × 16 = 40 oz.
